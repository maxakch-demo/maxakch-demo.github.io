Suction rubber hose

Flexible suction and pressure-suction pipes are designed for suction and injection of various liquids and liquids in the temperature range from -35 to +70 ° C. The suction pipe operates under vacuum to 0.08 MPa (600 mm Hg), the suction tube is pressurized at a pressure of 0.5; 1.0 MPa and a discharge of 0.08 MPa. Elastic pipes of this group differ from others in design, namely, the presence of a wire spiral and spacer rings, which provide resistance to deformation under local load, stability under the influence of external pressure and vacuum, and protects the inner layers from stratification.
