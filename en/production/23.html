Discharge Rubber Hose (TFP) are made of wear-resistant rubber and high-strength fabric layers of force, can be manufactured up to 10 meters long with flanged connections. The production of rubber pipes of different lengths makes it easy to use them in existing piping systems with minimal installation costs. The rubber pipeline consists of several sections of rubber-fabric (rubber) pipes and does not require specially trained personnel and a special tool. The operation of rubber pipelines in the open air or in a humid environment does not require additional measures to protect them from corrosion. Rubber pipes consist of an internal protective coating - rubbers, resistant to hydroabrasive wear, and strength layers of high-strength synthetic fabrics and protective coating, so these pipes are more correctly called rubber-fabric.

Rubber-fabric pipes are made from 89 to 1200 mm in diameter, up to 10 meters in length, at working pressure up to 4 MPa. Pipes can be manufactured in a floating version. Rubber pipes produced by "Rassvet-K" have a life of 5 to 8 times more water abrasive wear than steel pipes.

Rubber-fabric pipes have an operating temperature of -35 ° C to 70 ° C and a working pressure of 1.0 MPa *.
