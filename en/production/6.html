Flexible shells are intended for use as flexible prefabricated water-filling dams, water-filling dams, tanks for temporary storage of water and other liquids. Shells can be produced with dimensions up to 50 m in length and up to 5 m in diameter, for different purposes and for performance.

The development is protected by the Russian patent for invention No. 2291931.
