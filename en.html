About the company

Since 1995 Rassvet-K LLC has been developing and manufacturing wear-resistant rubber products for hydromechanization enterprises and mining and processing plants: flexible hoses, expansion joints, pipeline fittings and flexible casings.

  • Design and manufacturing

    Own design and research departments; products are collected in Kursk.

  • Wear resistance and patents

    Service life under hydroabrasive wear 5–8 times longer than steel pipes. 6 Russian patents.

  • Made to customer drawings

    Flow distributors, slurry dividers and pipeline lines to the customer’s drawings or schemes.

More about the company ▾

Rassvet-K LLC develops and manufactures wear-resistant rubber products for hydromechanization enterprises and mining and processing plants. The company was founded in 1995; its specialists have production, research and design experience.

Product range: flexible discharge, suction-discharge and suction hoses, flexible expansion joints, abrasion-resistant flow distributors, pipeline fittings (bends, tees), conical flexible reducers and flexible casings.

The design and research departments develop new products, tooling, non-standard equipment and manufacturing technology. Specialists of Gidrokomplekt LLC and Rassvet-K LLC carry out research on new product designs and manufacturing technologies.

We are open to cooperation with Russian and foreign companies and research organisations in creating and implementing new ideas, inventions and discoveries.

Articles

Learn more →

  • Prospects for the use of polymeric materials in hydromechanization

    The main ways to improve the efficiency of the hydromechanized complex are: reducing its material consumption and energy consumption, increasing abrasion and corrosion resistance, reducing operating costs.

  • Prefabricated water dams

    Water has always been a source of danger for humans. The water element, as a rule, is sudden and unpredictable, and measures to protect against it must be the same reactive. The enterprise "Rassvet-K" developed quickly erected flexible dams, which can be temporarily used for the liquidation of emergency situations (ES) for the period of the installation of basic protective structures, allowing to gain time for the evacuation of people from the disaster zone.

  • Flexible soil-holders

    Elastic floating subterranean lines are designed for hydrotransportation of soil during dredging, underwater trenching, hydro-washing of industrial sites, etc. Primer lines are manufactured in several modifications: pressure, pressure-suction and suction.
