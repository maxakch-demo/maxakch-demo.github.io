Description of rubber flange transitions

Wear-resistant flange transitions of elastic materials are designed for connecting pipelines of different diameters. The taper of the adapters ensures the smoothness of the flows, reduces the loss of pressure on friction, reduces the amount of hydroabrasive wear. Power layers made of high-strength synthetic fabrics provide high hydrostatic strength. Rubber transitions from elastic materials dampen the vibration that occurs when the pump is running. The presence of rotating flanges allows quick installation and dismantling of pipeline systems. Rubber-fiber adapters can be used together with tees for modular assembly of flow distributors and pulp separators.

Characteristics of rubber flanges

Rubber transitions flanged have a temperature limit of serviceability from -35 ° C to + 70 ° C, working pressure of 1 MPa, vacuum of 0,08 MPa.
